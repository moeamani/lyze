# AI Writing Tells, Complete Reference

Source basis: Grammarly, "Decoding AI Language: Common Words and Phrases in AI-Generated Content" (updated Feb 2026), rewritten and adapted.

## Part 1: The four structural characteristics of AI text

1. **Formal, robotic tone.** Same sentence structures repeating, low emotion, low nuance.
2. **Repetitive phrasing.** Pattern-prediction defaults to familiar phrases, cliches, and sometimes outdated info.
3. **No personal touch.** Synthesis and generalization without lived stories, feelings, or an original point of view.
4. **Predictable formatting.** Title-case headings, tidy bullet stacks, over-polished phrasing.

Mechanism: LLMs predict the statistically likely next word, so "safe" vocabulary recurs and becomes a fingerprint.

## Part 2: Word tables

### High-frequency AI words

| Word | What it means | Human swap |
|---|---|---|
| Delve into | investigate deeply | explore |
| Underscore | emphasize | highlight |
| Pivotal | crucial | important |
| Realm | field or domain | area |
| Harness | make use of | use |
| Illuminate | clarify | explain |

### Formulaic transitions

| Phrase | Job it does | Human swap |
|---|---|---|
| That being said | introduce contrast | however / even so |
| At its core | summarize essence | fundamentally / essentially |
| To put it simply | simplify | simply put, or just say it |
| This underscores the importance of | emphasize takeaway | this highlights the need for |
| A key takeaway is | summarize point | the main point is |
| From a broader perspective | zoom out | when you zoom out / on a larger scale |

### Hedging and softening words

Best fix is usually deletion, or replacement with a specific claim.

| Phrase | Job it does | Human swap |
|---|---|---|
| Generally speaking | generalize with exceptions | in most cases / as a rule / delete |
| Typically | mark the common case | usually / most often |
| Tends to | soft pattern claim | is often |
| Arguably | flag debatability | some may say / delete |
| To some extent | limit scope | partially / delete |
| Broadly speaking | generalize | overall / delete |

### Analytical and academic words

| Word | What it means | Human swap |
|---|---|---|
| Shed light on | clarify | explain / clarify |
| Facilitate | make easier | help / enable |
| Refine | improve gradually | improve |
| Bolster | strengthen | support / reinforce |
| Differentiate | distinguish | tell apart |
| Streamline | simplify a process | simplify |

### Overused buzzwords

Replace with a concrete specific, or cut entirely. "Innovative" and friends are filler.

| Word | Human fix |
|---|---|
| Revolutionize | transform, or name the actual change |
| Innovative | cut, or show the specific new thing |
| Cutting-edge | advanced, or cut |
| Game-changing | significant, or cut |
| Transformative | impactful, or describe the before/after |
| Seamless integration | works effortlessly with X, or name the integration |
| Scalable solution | expandable, or state actual capacity |

## Part 3: Humanizing strategies (from the source's FAQ)

1. Add personal insights or anecdotes.
2. Adjust tone and style away from the default register.
3. Trade formulaic structures for something more surprising or original.

## Part 4: Responsible AI use checklist

1. Keep human review at the center; edit output to match intent, voice, quality.
2. Be transparent about AI's role where norms require it; cite AI like any source.
3. Track what is self-written vs AI-assisted when integrity matters (e.g., academic or editorial work).
4. Use AI to enhance creativity, not replace it: AI output is a starting point.
5. Know the AI-use rules of the field you are writing for (work, school, publication).

## Part 5: Detection reality check

AI detectors are probabilistic estimates, not proof. Accuracy varies with model quality and how much the text was edited. Humans use every word above too; it is the density and combination of tells that signals AI, not any single word.
